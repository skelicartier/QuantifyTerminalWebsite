What we do

One system from evidence to decision.

Most tools stop at information. Quantify Terminal maintains the state between the event and the decision: the evidence, the model, the thesis and the portfolio.

01 · Evidence

A point-in-time data fabric.

Filings, prices, transcripts and macro prints enter one canonical system with source lineage, effective dates and machine-readable data rights. Every fact carries when it was true, when it was available and where it came from.

02 · Intelligence

Models and theses that stay current.

Filings compile into versioned financial models. Thesis assumptions are explicit, measurable and linked to the evidence that would invalidate them. Language models interpret and explain; deterministic code runs the numbers.

03 · Decision

Portfolio consequences, on record.

The Portfolio Digital Twin recomputes exposure and runs scenarios. The Decision Ledger records every recommendation, approval and outcome, so the reasoning behind a position can be replayed.

Why we built it

Information is not the hard part.

The hard part is maintaining the state between an event and a decision. That is the system we are building.

Terminals tell investors what happened. Quantify Terminal determines, on a cycle while it runs, what changed in the investor's evidence, assumptions, theses and risk because it happened.

Search, summarisation and generic AI research are baseline capabilities now. They do not maintain an investor's financial state. That state lives in spreadsheets, notes, models and memory, and it goes stale the moment the world moves.

Quantify Terminal is built as an event-to-decision system. Filings, earnings, news and macro prints enter the Data Fabric with canonical identifiers, point-in-time timestamps and machine-readable data rights; private documents stay in the owner's own library. Connectors write them as facts, and a model change carries the filed line that states it. The World Model traces the change through suppliers, customers, geographies and sectors. The Financial Model Compiler proposes cell-level updates with source lineage. The thesis graph re-scores every assumption. The Portfolio Digital Twin recomputes first-, second- and third-order exposure. The Decision Engine tests the result against the investor's constitution and writes the outcome to the Decision Ledger.

Every value the Fabric holds stores provenance, point-in-time state and the rights decisions made on it, and every memory write is recorded. Language models plan, interpret and explain. Financial mathematics runs in deterministic, versioned code. All of it surfaces inside a native desktop terminal on macOS, Windows and Linux.

We build the system that maintains conviction, not just information.

How we build

Principles, not promises.

The rules the system runs on. They are constraints in the architecture, not statements of intent.

Deterministic finance

Language models plan, interpret and explain. DCF, WACC, ROIC, factor, risk and backtest mathematics run in versioned code, never in prose.

Point-in-time by default

Every observation carries effective, available and ingested timestamps. Research and backtests use what was knowable at the time, not what was restated later.

Human gates on capital

Portfolio changes require explicit confirmation and an audit record. Live orders require hard confirmation, broker controls and a kill switch. Nothing trades by accident.

Evidence or nothing

Every agent argument cites evidence and deterministic calculations. Bull, bear, forensic, macro, industry, quant and risk perspectives use different evidence paths, and their disagreement stays visible.
