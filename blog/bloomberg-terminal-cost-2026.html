Bloomberg Terminal Cost in 2026

A single Bloomberg Terminal costs $31,980 a year in 2026, or $28,320 a terminal on a multi-terminal subscription. Here is what that buys, what it still leaves to your analysts, and when it is worth it.

By Aaryan Saroha, Founder and CEO · Published 31 August 2026 · Updated 27 September 2026 · 3 min read

$31,980 a year for one terminal, which is $2,665 a month, and $28,320 a year for each terminal on a multi-terminal subscription, which is $2,360 a month. Bloomberg sells two-year subscriptions and does not publish a rate card, so your contract can differ.

The price in 2026

Bloomberg raised the monthly price of two-year subscriptions by 6.5% for renewals from 1 January 2025, saying the increase is “linked to weighted global inflation over the prior two-year period” [1]. That took a single terminal to $31,980 a year and a multi-terminal seat from $26,580 to $28,320. Pricing trackers still showed $31,980 in August 2026 [2].

What the price buys

The terminal is the data and the network at once: prices and news across markets, fixed-income depth few rivals match, analytics, and Instant Bloomberg, the chat much of the market still negotiates over. For a desk that trades bonds or lives on that network, the price is the cost of being in the room.

What it still leaves to your analysts

A terminal is very good at telling you what happened. It is not built to tell you what it means for your positions. When a 10-Q lands overnight, someone still has to find the figures that changed, update the model, check whether the reason the fund owns the stock still holds, work out how much of the book it reaches, and write down what was decided and why.

At most funds that work is done by hand every morning. It is the part of the cost that never appears on the invoice.

Where Quantify Terminal fits

Is a Bloomberg Terminal worth it?

Yes, if you trade fixed income, depend on Instant Bloomberg to reach counterparties, or need the breadth for a large team. It is harder to justify when the work is public-equity research and portfolio monitoring for a few people, where a research platform and one or two specialists cover most of the week. Frequently asked questions

How much is a Bloomberg Terminal per month?

$2,665 a month for a single terminal and $2,360 a month for each terminal on a multi-terminal subscription, on a two-year subscription.

Does Bloomberg give discounts?

The multi-terminal rate is lower than the single-terminal rate, and larger contracts are negotiated. Bloomberg does not publish its discounts.

Why is a Bloomberg Terminal so expensive?

You pay for the data, the network and the support together, per seat, on a two-year subscription, and Bloomberg ties its increases to inflation over the prior two years.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Bloomberg?

For research and portfolio monitoring, yes: Koyfin, TIKR and TradingView have free and paid tiers, and FactSet and LSEG Workspace are the enterprise alternatives. None replaces Bloomberg's network.
