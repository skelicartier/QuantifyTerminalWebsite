Product

One system from new information to decision.

When a filing lands, the change it makes to your model is proposed with the source attached to every number, the assumptions your thesis rests on are re-checked, and your exposure is recomputed — and what you decide is recorded, so a year later you can prove why you acted.

A full desk underneath: markets, research, screeners, options, bonds, funds, macro, geopolitics, portfolio and risk.

The eight systems

One event bus, eight systems on it.

Each reads the same evidence graph, model registry, thesis graph and Portfolio Twin, and each writes back with provenance, point-in-time state, permissions, model version and an audit event.

01 Pulse

Pulse scores your holdings and watchlists every five minutes while the terminal is open: the move against its volatility, volume against its 20-day median, the opening gap, the range against its average true range, deduplicated headline count and its rate of change, earnings proximity or surprise, consensus FY1 EPS revisions, a change to filed guidance, and encyclopedia readership. Names you hold, watch or have a thesis on rank higher.

An index-wide move holds back the single-name move signals it explains. Each signal opens to what every measured input contributed, its evidence, and what changed since your last sign-in. Macro prints, unrest reported where your holdings operate and broken thesis assumptions are separate signals; month-end short interest is context, not a score.

02 Deep Research

You pick a question, a mode and the tickers. The mode's fixed plan collects quotes, fundamentals, filings, estimates, macro prints and your notes and documents as numbered evidence, then computes growth, margins, ROIC, leverage, accounting-quality scores, cost of equity, WACC, three DCF cases and the growth the price implies. Anything missing an input is skipped and says so; where two sources report a figure differently, both are shown, never averaged.

Every number must match a cited item; sentences whose numbers do not are removed and become open questions. The rest keeps its citations, calculations and a confidence figure, and is saved to Memory, where an amended filing marks it stale.

03 Financial Model Compiler

The compiler proposes cells for thirteen statement lines from the figures a company tagged in its own filings, or from a data vendor's copy. It writes nothing. Each proposal names its source and filing version, and a changed cell shows both values. An amended filing is marked a restatement, not a change.

You approve or reject each cell; a rejection is remembered. Approving commits a new version, keeps the previous one readable, and re-checks any thesis reading the cells that moved. Eleven named calculations recompute margins, free cash flow, net debt, returns and growth; a missing input leaves no cell. Assumptions fill periods no filing reports, never a reported figure.

04 Thesis and Health

You write the thesis: title, direction, horizon and the assumptions under it. Each assumption is a numeric condition — threshold, weight, period — on any field the Data Fabric carries, or on a cell in a model you committed. Quantify Terminal re-checks them roughly daily, and again when a model commit moves a cell they read, marking each holding, warning, broken or unchecked. Health is the weighted score of the ones it could check, shown beside each assumption's status and last value, and left blank rather than zero when it could check none. A break reaches Pulse with the value that broke it; two broken on two checks running invalidate the thesis.

05 Portfolio Digital Twin

The book is positions with weights, sector, country and currency, held against what the strategy says. A filing on one holding carries the share of the book it reaches, counting the names measurably moving with it. Liquidity is days to exit at twenty per cent of twenty-day average volume. What it cannot classify or measure, it names.

A shock search cuts a company model's inputs by ten, twenty and thirty per cent, alone and in pairs, ranked by the fall in free cash flow — a model line, not a price — times the book that company reaches. Hedging prices puts, proxies and selling down for one holding against a fall you name, on cost, carry, coverage and basis risk. Nothing is ordered.

06 Decision Ledger and Replay

The decision form records the name, the action, the rationale, the evidence, report and calculation cited, the weight before and after, the expected return range, the risks named, and the model and thesis version on screen. A mandate written as rules is checked as you type, and a breach saves only with a signed override.

Replay reads back that model version, that thesis version and every field the thesis watched as it stood then, and names what was not kept. Any decision exports as a checksummed evidence pack. Outcome attribution splits a thesis's return into market beta and residual, and into multiple change and earnings, or says why it cannot. Currency is not separated.

07 Scenario Lab and Red Team

Shock a model cell by a percentage; every derived cell rebuilds through the same named recipes — margins, free cash flow, growth — and nothing is saved. The World Model walks supplier, subsidiary, issuer and fund edges to the holdings reached, naming each route in words, each step filed or inferred; every model commit runs the same trace. A failure search ranks the single and paired shocks that break a line, not a price.

Convene seven seats, each on one evidence path: bull, bear, forensic, macro, industry, quant, risk. Each takes a side, or names what it could not read and takes none. Nothing is averaged.

It argues. It does not decide, it writes nothing, and it never places an order.

08 Agent access — MCP, Python and spreadsheets

Ten verbs answer over MCP, or from Python against the same server. Eight read: a company; canonical fields with their provenance; its filings, most material first, with the sentence to quote; a thesis scored; exposure through the book; what a recompile would propose; the seven seats; what hedging a fall costs. Two draft or check a plan and mark what needs a person; neither runs one. Nothing writes, and nothing is ordered. The same rights, point-in-time and audit checks apply whichever door you use.

The server is set up on request, not shipped in the download. The terminal has its own spreadsheet and the QT formulas answer in it with nothing deployed; for Excel and Google Sheets the connector is yours to deploy and key, and it returns market data, not canonical fields. Agent access →

Foundation

Built on the Data Fabric.

Every market observation, corporate fact, filing claim, event, relationship and private document carries a canonical entity, a source, a licence profile, point-in-time timestamps and a confidence state. The desk never depends on which vendor supplied a field. Every source carries a licence profile, and entitlements are enforced at retrieval. How we handle data rights →

Point-in-time observation 
effective_at    period or instant the fact describes
published_at    when the source published it
available_at    earliest time Quantify Terminal could have known it
ingested_at     when Quantify Terminal received it
valid_from / valid_to   version interval for restatements
source_version  immutable vendor / document fingerprint

Entity resolution

Tickers, ISINs, LEIs, CIKs, exchange symbols, aliases and company-name history resolve to one canonical ID.

No look-ahead

Historical research and backtests read available_at, never the filing-period date, so restated numbers cannot leak into the past.

Rights enforcement

Display, non-display, derived, cache, redistribution and AI-retrieval rights are machine-readable per source and applied by the read layer on every request.

World Model

Companies, securities, people, facilities, suppliers, customers, funds, countries, regulators and commodities connect as temporal, confidence-weighted edges.

What it does differently

Four things a terminal leaves to you.

A terminal will tell you what happened. These four are the difference between knowing that and knowing what it means for the position you are holding.

The thesis object

Your thesis, as a live object

Not a memo in a folder. Assumptions with thresholds — gross margin stays above 42%, hyperscaler capex grows above 20% — each linked to the evidence supporting it, the model cells driving it, and the size of the position depending on it. Versioned, so you can see exactly what you believed in March.

Provenance

Every number carries the sentence it came from

Every material figure traces to a source or a deterministic calculation. Reported facts, your assumptions, consensus and our estimates stay visibly separate. Nothing is silently overwritten — material changes arrive as a diff you approve.

Role templates

Set up for your job, not for everyone’s

Start from a desk built for how you work — credit, EM macro, options, fundamental long-only — with the panels arranged and the thesis layer configured. Everything else is still there when you want it.
