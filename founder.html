Why we built Quantify Terminal

Terminals are very good at telling you what happened. What it means for your book is still worked out by hand: someone reads the filing, re-types the number into the model, and checks whether the reason for owning the position still holds. On a busy morning that work gets skipped, and that is where the expensive mistakes are.

Quantify Terminal is built to do that work and to show it. When a filing lands, the change it makes to your model is proposed with the sentence it came from. The thesis that depends on it is re-checked, the exposure is recomputed, and what you decide is recorded with everything that was known at the time. The mathematics runs in deterministic, versioned code, and every number traces to its source.

We are building it as infrastructure for investment teams: fast, transparent, and plain about what it cannot see.
